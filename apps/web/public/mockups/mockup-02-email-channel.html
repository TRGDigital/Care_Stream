Here is a summary of the Medication Administration Policy, with the controlled drugs section highlighted.

Policy Summary

The Medication Administration Policy sets out the procedures all care staff must follow when handling, recording, and administering medications, with particular provisions for controlled drugs under the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2001.

Key Points — Controlled Drugs
Two-person verification required for all controlled drug administration.
CD register must be completed immediately before and after each administration.
Stock counts must be reconciled at every shift handover.
Any discrepancy must be reported to the manager and pharmacist immediately.
The CD register and medication record must both be signed by the administering carer and a witness — never completed retrospectively.
What this means in practice

Always complete the CD register at the time of administration. If a discrepancy is found, stop all further administration and contact your manager immediately.
