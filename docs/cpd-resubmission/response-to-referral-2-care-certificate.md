Dear Kerri,

Thank you, and please pass our thanks to the assessor for confirming that most
of the original referral points are now satisfied. We have addressed each of
the remaining points below.

**1. ICO number**

The ICO registration number, ZC221613 (TRG Digital Ltd), has now been entered
and saved in the member details section of CPD SMART, and we have checked that
it displays there.

**4b. Description**

The description in CPD SMART has been replaced in full. It now refers to all 16
Care Certificate standards (March 2025) and describes the current course
structure: one section per standard with a lesson, workplace scenario,
interactive activity and knowledge check; the pre-course check; the 32 question
final assessment; the feedback and reflection step; the observed workplace
assessment by the employer; and the 1.5 hour learning time. No reference to 15
standards remains.

**4c. Target audience and entry requirements**

The target audience section in CPD SMART has been updated to read: "For health
and social care staff who have already completed the Care Certificate
induction, or who hold equivalent knowledge and experience from their care
role. This is the annual refresher of that induction, not a replacement for it.
No formal entry qualifications are needed."

**5c. Verification and quality assurance**

Thank you for the clarification, which we understand and accept. The CPD
Certification Service assesses and certifies the learning value and educational
quality of the training material; it does not accredit individuals, products
or organisations. A CPD certificate we issue is evidence that the learner
completed the course and passed our assessment. It is not a government
recognised qualification and does not certify competence to perform a
particular role, treatment or activity.

To make sure our materials say the same, we have made the following changes:

- Every certificate now carries this statement: "This certificate is evidence
  that the named person completed this course and passed CareStream's
  assessment. It is not a government recognised qualification and does not
  certify competence to perform a particular role, treatment or activity."
- Where a course includes an observed assessment, the certificate now states
  that it was carried out by the employer, and names who signed it off and
  when. It is recorded as the employer's own assessment of workplace practice,
  not as something the CPD certificate confirms.
- The observation checklist, the knowledge test and assessment document and the learning
  outcomes have been reworded in the same way. The final learning outcome now
  reads: "Show your knowledge in the final assessment, and apply it in practice
  during an observed assessment by your employer."
- The certificate will only carry the CPD Certified mark, and the heading "CPD
  Approved Course", once certification has been granted.

The updated delegate certificate and knowledge test and assessment documents are uploaded with
this response.

Kind regards,

Chief Executive, CareStream
